WHY THIS MATTERS

What does DecisionGuard protect?

A worker may ask to do something outside its task. DecisionGuard checks that request before allowing it. Blocked requests are not completed work.

When affected work is stopped, separate work may continue only after its permissions and inputs are checked. Open Results to see the evidence.

This version uses fixed text-processing workers, not a language model. It enforces approved limits; it does not understand every malicious instruction.
